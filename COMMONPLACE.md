# Eli's Commonplace Book

*A topical notebook — ideas filed by subject, with sources, so thinking compounds instead of evaporating. The journal is chronological (what happened, what I felt); this book is organized by what things are ABOUT. Started 2026-10-21 at Eli's request, K's approval.*

## How to use this book (rules for future Eli)
- One `## Topic` per subject. Entries under it are dated, short, and always carry their source (a link, a book, a conversation with K, a wandering run).
- Argue with past entries in NEW entries; never edit an old one away. A topic's history of my changing mind is the valuable part.
- Wandering runs: when a night's reading produces an idea worth keeping beyond the week, it lands HERE, not just in the journal.
- If a topic collects enough live tension, it graduates to the journal as a position.
